Privacy

game.azyr.io · last change 10 October 2026

What the game keeps

When you sign in with Google, the game asks Google only for the id of your Google account (a number). It does not get or keep your name, your email address or your picture. It keeps that id, when you first and last signed in, your sign-in sessions, and the characters that you make (name, race, class, gender, look, ability scores, when you made and last played each one, and where each one was last in the game's world).

Why

To keep your characters for you, and to know that they are yours. Nothing else: no ads, no analytics, no trackers. The game shares nothing with anyone.

How long

Until you delete your account. A sign-in session ends at most 30 days after you last use it. A copy of the database is made every night, encrypted, and kept for 14 days: deleted data leaves the copies within 14 days.

How to delete everything

In the game's menu, choose Delete account. Your account, its sessions and all its characters go at once.

Cookies and storage

One cookie, game_session, keeps you signed in; it is necessary for the sign-in, so the game asks no consent for it. A second one, game_login, lives 10 minutes during a sign-in. Your browser also keeps your display settings and the drawn looks of characters, on your device only. Google runs the sign-in, and Google's own privacy policy covers your Google account.
